Questions常见问题

Is the chat really free?咨询真的免费吗？

Yes. The first consultation is free, with no obligation afterwards.是的。初步咨询免费，之后也没有任何义务。

Who do I pay?费用付给谁？

The school or its official partner, directly. We don’t collect tuition, deposits or application fees.直接付给学校或其官方合作方。我们不收取学费、押金或申请费。

Can you guarantee an offer?能保证录取吗？

No. Only the school decides, and we never sign admission guarantees.不能。录取只由学校决定，我们不签署任何录取担保协议。

Do you apply or get the visa for me?你们代办申请或签证吗？

No. We explain the steps so you know what to expect; you apply to the school and for the visa yourself.不代办。我们会讲清流程，申请和签证由你本人完成。

We do not handle admissions, collect fees from students, or sign admission guarantee agreements. Students register, pay and complete all procedures directly with the school or its overseas partner.
