# 英国升学路径 · Routes into UK education

各阶段简要介绍，具体要求以各校公布为准。Every school sets its own requirements.

## 寄宿学校与 GCSE · Boarding school and GCSEs（13–16 岁）
不少国际学生在 13 或 14 岁进入英国寄宿学校，16 岁参加 GCSE 考试。学校通常考英语和数学，并常有面试。
Many international students join a UK boarding school at 13 or 14 and take GCSEs at 16. Schools usually test English and maths, and often interview.

- 常见入学：Year 9（13 岁）或 Year 10（14 岁） · Typical entry: Year 9 (age 13) or Year 10 (age 14)
- 学制：2–3 年完成 GCSE · Length: 2–3 years to GCSE
- 申请方式：直接向学校申请；入学测试 + 面试 · How you apply: Directly to each school; entry tests and interview
- 未成年人：通常需要在英国的监护人 · Under 18s: A UK guardian is normally required
- 建议准备：提前 12–24 个月 · Start planning: 12–24 months ahead

适合：希望孩子尽早完全融入英式教育的家庭。
Good fit for: parents who want an early, full immersion in British schooling.

## Sixth Form：A-Level 或 IB · Sixth form: A-levels or IB（16–18 岁）
两年 A-Level（通常选 3–4 门）或 IB 课程，是进入英国大学最直接的路径。
Two years of A-levels (usually three or four subjects) or the IB Diploma. This is the most direct route to a UK university.

- 常见入学：16 岁，GCSE 后或国内高一后 · Typical entry: Age 16, after GCSEs or Chinese Grade 10
- 学制：2 年 · Length: 2 years
- 申请方式：直接向学校或学院申请 · How you apply: Directly to each school or college
- 未成年人：通常需要在英国的监护人 · Under 18s: A UK guardian is normally required
- 建议准备：提前 12–18 个月 · Start planning: 12–18 months ahead

适合：目标是英国名校的学生。
Good fit for: students aiming at competitive UK universities.

## 国际本科预科 · International foundation year（17–19 岁）
为国内高中毕业生设计的一年过渡课程，提升学术英语和专业基础，之后衔接本科一年级。
A one-year bridge for students finishing Chinese high school. It builds academic English and subject knowledge, then leads on to year one of a degree.

- 常见入学：国内高三毕业后 · Typical entry: After Chinese high school (Grade 12)
- 学制：1 年（部分为 6 个月） · Length: 1 year (some 6-month options)
- 申请方式：向大学或其官方预科合作方申请 · How you apply: To the university or its official foundation partner
- 语言要求：常见雅思 4.5–5.5，视课程而定 · English: Often IELTS 4.5–5.5, depending on the course
- 建议准备：提前 6–12 个月 · Start planning: 6–12 months ahead

适合：高中成绩或英语需要一年过渡的学生。
Good fit for: students whose high school results or English need a bridge year.

## 本科学位 · Undergraduate degree（18+ 岁）
英格兰本科大多为 3 年，苏格兰为 4 年。通过 UCAS 系统申请，最多 5 个志愿，提交一份个人陈述。
Most bachelor’s degrees in England take three years, and four in Scotland. Applications go through UCAS, with up to five choices and one personal statement.

- 常见入学：A-Level、IB、预科后；部分大学接受高考成绩 · Typical entry: After A-levels, IB, a foundation year, or strong gaokao at some universities
- 学制：3 年（英格兰），4 年（苏格兰） · Length: 3 years (England), 4 years (Scotland)
- 申请方式：UCAS 英国大学申请系统 · How you apply: UCAS, the UK admissions service
- 语言要求：通常雅思 6.0–7.0 · English: Usually IELTS 6.0–7.0
- 建议准备：提前 12–18 个月 · Start planning: 12–18 months ahead

适合：已明确专业方向的学生。
Good fit for: students ready to choose a subject and commit to it.

## 一年制授课型硕士 · One-year taught master’s（21+ 岁）
英国授课型硕士大多为 1 年。直接向各大学申请，部分热门专业名额满得早。
Most UK taught master’s degrees take one year. You apply directly to each university, and some popular courses fill early.

- 常见入学：本科毕业后（国内学位普遍认可） · Typical entry: After a bachelor’s degree (Chinese degrees widely accepted)
- 学制：通常 12 个月 · Length: Usually 12 months
- 申请方式：在线直接向各大学申请 · How you apply: Directly to each university, online
- 语言要求：通常雅思 6.5–7.0 · English: Usually IELTS 6.5–7.0
- 建议准备：提前 9–15 个月 · Start planning: 9–15 months ahead

适合：想用一年时间高效深造的本科毕业生。
Good fit for: graduates who want a fast, focused next step.

## 短期课程与成人进修 · Short courses and adult study（不限年龄 · any age）
暑期学校、语言课程和短期职业课程，从几周到几个月不等，是先体验英国的好方式。
Summer schools, English courses and short professional programmes, from a few weeks to a few months. A good way to try the UK first.

- 常见入学：青少年（暑期学校）与成人 · Typical entry: Teenagers (summer schools) and adults
- 学制：2 周至 6 个月 · Length: 2 weeks to 6 months
- 申请方式：直接向学校或课程机构报名 · How you apply: Directly to the school or course provider
- 语言要求：各级别均有课程 · English: Courses for every level
- 建议准备：提前 3–6 个月 · Start planning: 3–6 months ahead

适合：先体验英国，或不读完整学位也想提升职业能力。
Good fit for: a first taste of the UK, or a career boost without a full degree.
