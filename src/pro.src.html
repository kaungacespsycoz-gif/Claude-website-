Common questions常见问题

Is the first consultation free?初步咨询免费吗？

Yes, with no obligation afterwards.免费，之后也没有任何义务。

Who do I pay?费用付给谁？

The school or its official partner, directly. We do not collect tuition, deposits or application fees.直接付给学校或其官方合作方。我们不收取学费、押金或申请费。

Can you guarantee an offer?能保证录取吗？

No. Admission decisions are made only by the school, and we do not sign admission guarantees.不能。录取只由学校决定，我们不签署任何录取担保协议。

Do you handle applications or visas?你们代办申请或签证吗？

No. We explain the process so you know what to expect; you apply to the school and for the visa yourself.不代办。我们会讲清流程，申请和签证由你本人完成。

Oriwis provides study-abroad information matching, preliminary consultations and referrals only. We are not a study-abroad agency.
